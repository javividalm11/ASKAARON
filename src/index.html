GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Before you
Ask Aaron.

Is Aaron my contractor?+

No. Ask Aaron provides a construction second opinion to help you understand the documents and questions behind your decision. Your contractor remains responsible for the construction work.

What should I send for a review?+

Send your proposal or estimate, written scope, allowances, exclusions, and any supporting plans or photos. For bid comparisons, include two or three proposals. For change orders, include the original scope too.

How much does a review cost?+

The initial 15-minute consultation is free. Aaron will confirm the scope and fee for a document review before you decide to proceed.

Can I talk to Aaron before uploading documents?+

Absolutely. Request your free consultation first. Uploading documents is optional, so you can start wherever you feel comfortable.

Does this replace an inspection or legal advice?+

No. A document review is not a site inspection, engineering assessment, legal opinion, or guarantee of contractor performance. You may still need qualified professionals for those services.
